MY STORY

Chasing stories, not destinations.

My journey began with a fascination for motorcycles and the freedom they represent. Over time, that passion evolved into filmmaking—capturing places, people and emotions through the lens.

Whether it's creating a commercial film, documenting an expedition through the Himalayas, or telling a brand's story, I believe authenticity is what makes every frame memorable.
